# MCM 2023 Problem A — Structured Modeling Report

## Problem framing

The case asks how a plant community evolves under irregular drought cycles, how biodiversity changes resilience, and how drought regime, species composition, pollution, and habitat reduction alter long-run viability. The supplied benchmark contains the official two-page problem text and no tabular attachment rows (`data_files=[]`, `data_audit=[]`); therefore all numerical outputs below are generated by an explicit synthetic mechanism and are not fitted to omitted observations.

## Assumptions

Annual time steps represent one generation. Each species has fixed drought tolerance and intrinsic growth sampled once from transparent ranges. Total biomass is limited by a carrying capacity reduced multiplicatively by pollution and habitat loss. Biodiversity provides a complementarity bonus that saturates as `(N-1)/N`. Drought occurrence is Bernoulli; conditional severity is truncated Gaussian. Biomass is non-negative and updated with an exponential discrete logistic step.

## Candidate models

1. A stochastic discrete multispecies logistic model with species-specific drought response (selected): interpretable, stable, supports irregular forcing and counterfactuals.
2. A continuous Lotka–Volterra competition ODE with time-varying precipitation: more mechanistic but requires unobserved interaction coefficients and a numerical ODE dependency. Because no attachment data are available, it is retained as a qualitative alternative rather than implemented.

## Baseline and math specification

For species `i=1,…,N`, biomass `B_i(t)≥0`, total `S_t=Σ_i B_i(t)`, drought indicator `D_t~Bernoulli(p)`, and severity `z_t=max(0,min(1, Normal(0.65, (0.65v)^2)))` when `D_t=1`, define precipitation `P_t=1-z_t` in drought and `P_t≈1` otherwise. Tolerance `τ_i∈[0.35,0.90]`, growth `r_i∈[0.72,0.90]`, capacity `K=K_0(1-ρ)(1-h)[1+0.12(N-1)/N]`. The update is

`B_i(t+1)=max(0, B_i(t) exp(r_i [1-z_t(1-τ_i)] [1-S_t/K]))`.

Outputs are final biomass, minimum biomass, drought-year mean biomass, and extinction flag (`final<10^-6`). Baseline parameters are `N∈{1,2,4,8}`, `p=0.30`, `v=0.25`, `ρ=h=0`, 40 years, seed 2023.

## Experiment and validation

The experiment compares richness, no-drought versus high-drought regimes, and combined pollution/habitat pressure. Validation is structural: repeated seeded runs are identical; state remains finite and non-negative; an extreme no-drought case is included; and scenarios use the same seed and horizon. With no observations, empirical calibration and out-of-sample validation are pending.

## Sensitivity/robustness

Counterfactuals vary drought frequency from 0.0 to 0.65, severity variation from 0.25 to 0.40, and apply 25% pollution plus 25% habitat loss. The model is monotone in the carrying-capacity penalty by construction. A full global sensitivity analysis is pending because the benchmark supplies no calibrated parameter distributions.

## Falsification

The biodiversity mechanism would be falsified if replicated calibrated data showed no richness-resilience relationship, a negative relationship after controlling for climate, or strong non-monotonicity inconsistent with the saturating complementarity term. The drought mechanism would be falsified by biomass increasing systematically with observed drought severity at fixed richness and pollution.

## Reviewer risks

Main risks are synthetic, uncalibrated parameters; annual discretization; a single interaction proxy instead of measured pairwise competition; and the absence of attachment observations. Results should be interpreted as a transparent scenario model, not empirical forecasts.
