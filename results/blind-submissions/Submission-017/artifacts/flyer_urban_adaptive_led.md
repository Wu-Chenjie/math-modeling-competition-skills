# LIGHT THE WAY, NOT THE SKY

## Urban community action flyer

### Adaptive, shielded LED lighting

Use light where people need it, at the brightness they need, for only as long as they need it.

**Three actions**

1. Aim fixtures downward and add full cut-off shielding.
2. Replace over-bright lamps with warm, adaptive LEDs and occupancy controls.
3. Dim nonessential commercial and roadway lighting during low-activity hours while preserving safe routes.

**Why it matters**

Shielding reduces skyglow, glare, and light trespass. Adaptive controls reduce unnecessary exposure while keeping high-use areas visible. The scenario model estimates the urban archetype score falling from **81.14** to **55.41** under this strategy (a **25.73-point** reduction).

**Measure and adjust**

Track sky brightness, complaints about glare/trespass, energy use, nighttime collisions, and ecological indicators. Review results quarterly and restore brightness only where safety evidence requires it.

*This is a one-page communication prototype for the supplied urban archetype. Values are assumption-based because the benchmark provides no field observations; validate locally before deployment.*
