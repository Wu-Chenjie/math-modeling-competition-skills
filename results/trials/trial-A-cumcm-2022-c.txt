**Problem framing**

1. Estimate associations between weathering, glass type, decoration, color, and composition; infer pre-weathering composition from paired/point measurements.
2. Classify known high-potassium versus lead-barium glass, then identify defensible subtypes within each class.
3. Classify the eight unknowns and quantify uncertainty.
4. Compare within-class and between-class compositional dependence structures.

Repeated samples from one artifact are not independent observations; artifact ID should be a grouping factor.

**Three candidate model families**

1. **Compositional discriminant family**  
   Replace raw percentages by an isometric log-ratio (ilr) transform, then fit regularized multinomial logistic regression, LDA/QDA, or nearest-centroid classification. Suitable for the main type label and interpretable element contrasts.

2. **Probabilistic compositional mixture family**  
   Fit class-conditional logistic-normal or Dirichlet mixtures in ilr space. Use mixture components as candidate subtypes, with model selection by held-out log-likelihood/BIC and stability under resampling.

3. **Constrained weathering and dependence family**  
   Estimate unweathered composition using a constrained mass-balance model; analyze associations with repeated-measures logistic/ordinal models and compare class-specific sparse precision matrices or log-ratio covariance matrices. This family addresses Questions 1 and 4 without violating closure.

**Baseline**

Unexecuted pending implementation:

- Treat blanks as zero for a deliberately simple reference model, then closure-normalize each row to sum to one.
- Classify using Euclidean nearest centroid on normalized compositions, with artifact-level grouped cross-validation.

**Mathematical specification**

Let \(x_{ij}\ge 0\) be measured proportion of component \(j\) for sample \(i\), and \(s_i=\sum_jx_{ij}\).

Validity indicator:

\[
v_i=\mathbf 1(85\le s_i\le105).
\]

After explicit zero/censoring policy and closure normalization:

\[
p_{ij}=x_{ij}/\sum_kx_{ik},\qquad
z_i=\operatorname{ilr}(p_i).
\]

Type model:

\[
\Pr(Y_i=c\mid z_i,w_i)
=\frac{\exp(\alpha_c+\beta_c^\top z_i+\gamma_c^\top w_i)}
{\sum_{r}\exp(\alpha_r+\beta_r^\top z_i+\gamma_r^\top w_i)},
\]

where \(w_i\) contains weathering, decoration, and color, and artifact-level random effects or grouped folds account for repeated measurements.

Subtype model: within each known class, fit

\[
z_i\mid g_i=k\sim N(\mu_k,\Sigma_k),\qquad
\Pr(g_i=k)=\pi_k,
\]

with \(K\) selected by held-out likelihood/BIC and stability.

Weathering reconstruction:

\[
\hat p_i^{\,0}
=\arg\min_{p\in\Delta^{D-1}}
\|A p-y_i\|_{W}^{2}
+\lambda R(p),
\]

subject to \(p_j\ge0\), \(\sum_jp_j=1\), and a specified exchange/removal matrix \(A\). The paired unweathered/severely weathered observations should identify or constrain \(A\); otherwise the reconstruction is only partially identifiable.

Association comparison should use log-ratio covariance or precision matrices, with bootstrap confidence intervals and permutation tests that preserve class labels and artifact grouping.

**Validation plan**

- Use artifact-grouped nested cross-validation; never split paired samples across train and test.
- Compare baseline and candidate families using balanced accuracy, macro-F1, log loss, calibration error, and confusion matrices.
- Bootstrap artifact IDs, not individual rows.
- Perform sensitivity runs for:
  - blank-as-zero versus censored/left-limit treatment;
  - raw closure versus ilr/robust ilr;
  - inclusion/exclusion of the two invalid known rows;
  - weathered versus unweathered-only training;
  - alternative subtype counts and random seeds.
- For unknowns, report probability intervals and classify as indeterminate when uncertainty overlaps both classes.

**Falsification questions**

- Does type prediction remain strong after removing PbO/BaO/K2O, or is it driven by one obvious component?
- Do results change materially when blanks are treated as censored rather than zero?
- Are apparent weathering effects explained by artifact identity or sampling location?
- Are subtype clusters stable under bootstrap, perturbation, and alternative transforms?
- Does the classifier outperform a composition-sum or dominant-component rule?
- Are class-specific correlations genuine after accounting for closure and multiple testing?
- Can the weathering inversion recover held-out unweathered points from paired data?

**Reproducibility risks**

- Missing chemical headers in the machine-readable header cells require independent verification.
- Blank-cell semantics and detection limits are undocumented.
- Two known rows fail the stated validity threshold and need row-level identification before modeling.
- Repeated artifact samples create leakage risk.
- Weathering inversion may be non-identifiable without explicit exchange assumptions.
- Small and imbalanced labeled data limit reliable subtype discovery.
- Exact preprocessing, ilr basis, random seeds, fold assignments, and software versions must be recorded.
