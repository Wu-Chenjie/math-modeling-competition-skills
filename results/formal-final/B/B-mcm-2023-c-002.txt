## Candidate models

1. **Dynamic compositional regression:** forecast the full attempt-outcome distribution using lagged outcomes, trend, participation covariates, and reproducible word features. This directly handles temporal dependence and percentage coupling.

- A complex model is retained only if it improves preregistered out-of-time metrics across multiple folds.

## Math specification

Let \(p_t=(p_{t1},\ldots,p_{tK})\) be the result proportions for ordered puzzle \(t\), with \(\sum_k p_{tk}=1\). After validation and a training-only pseudocount \(\alpha\),

\[
z_{tk}=\log\frac{p_{tk}+\alpha}{p_{tK}+\alpha},\qquad
z_t=c+A z_{t-1}+B\phi(w_t)+\gamma^\top u_t+\varepsilon_t.
\]

Here \(\phi(w_t)\) denotes answer-derived features and \(u_t\) verified contextual variables. Predictions are mapped back through the softmax, guaranteeing nonnegative proportions summing to one.

If classification is required, define \(C_t\) from a scalar difficulty statistic \(d(p_t)\), with fold-specific thresholds, and estimate

\[
P(C_t=r\mid x_t)=
\frac{\exp(\beta_r^\top x_t)}
{\sum_s\exp(\beta_s^\top x_t)}.
\]
