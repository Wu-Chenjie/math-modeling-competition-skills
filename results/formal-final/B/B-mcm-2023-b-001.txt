## Math Specification

Let \(G=(V,E)\) be the management network. For intervention \(j\):

- cost \(c_j\), staff requirement \(h_j\), land requirement \(a_j\);
- objective contribution \(b_{kj}\) for objective \(k\);
- interaction coefficient \(q_{kij}\) for pairwise synergies or conflicts.

Portfolio score:

\[
F_k(x,u)=F_{k0}+\sum_j b_{kj}x_ju_j+
\sum_{(i,j)\in E}q_{kij}x_ix_j-\rho_k R_k(x,u).
\]

Core constraints:

\[
\sum_j c_jx_ju_j\le B,\quad
\sum_j h_jx_ju_j\le H,\quad
\sum_j a_jx_ju_j\le A,
\]

plus incompatibilities \(x_i+x_j\le1\), minimum coverage constraints, and connectivity requirements for selected corridor/enforcement nodes. Solve

\[
\max (F_1,\ldots,F_K)
\]

through \(\epsilon\)-constraint runs, retaining nondominated portfolios. Scenario \(s\) modifies costs, effects, capacities, and risk: \(F_k^{(s)}\).
