## Assumptions

1. Species biomass or cover is nonnegative and bounded by a community carrying capacity.
2. Drought is represented by a known, piecewise-continuous forcing function \(D(t)\ge 0\).
3. Species differ in drought sensitivity and competitive effects.
4. Adaptation occurs between generations through heritable change in drought tolerance.
5. Initial total biomass is held fixed when comparing richness, avoiding a richness-versus-abundance confound.
6. Extinction is declared when biomass falls below a specified threshold \(\varepsilon\), to be set from the full problem specification.

## Candidate Models

**Model 1: Mechanistic ODE plus generation update.**  
For species \(i=1,\ldots,S\), biomass \(x_i(t)\) and tolerance \(a_i(g)\in[0,1]\):

\[
\frac{dx_i}{dt}
=x_i\left[r_i\left(1-\frac{\sum_j\alpha_{ij}x_j}{K}\right)
-m_i-\beta_iD(t)(1-a_i(g))\right].
\]

At generation \(g\), update tolerance using drought exposure \(E_g\):

\[
a_i(g+1)=\operatorname{clip}\left(a_i(g)+h_iE_g-c_i,\;0,\;1\right),
\qquad
E_g=\int_{gT}^{(g+1)T}D(t)\,dt .
\]

This directly represents competition, drought mortality, and adaptation.

**Model 2: Stochastic scenario simulation.**  
Use the same mechanism, but draw drought severity, duration, and species parameters from prespecified distributions. Estimate persistence, recovery, and \(S^\*\) by Monte Carlo. This assesses uncertainty rather than serving as an independent biological theory.

## Baseline

One species (\(S=1\)), fixed tolerance (\(h_1=0\)), identical initial total biomass, and the same drought forcing. Compare every richer community against this baseline using recovery, cumulative biomass, and extinction probability.

## Math Specification

For a specified benefit margin \(\Delta\) and acceptable extinction probability \(p_0\),

\[
S^\*=\min\left\{S:
A(S)-A(1)\ge\Delta,\quad
P_{\mathrm{ext}}(S)\le p_0\right\},
\]

where \(A(S)\) is a prespecified adaptation or recovery metric. Candidate metrics include post-drought biomass ratio, time to recovery, cumulative biomass, and tolerance increase. \(\Delta,p_0,\varepsilon,K,T\), and parameter distributions are pending.
