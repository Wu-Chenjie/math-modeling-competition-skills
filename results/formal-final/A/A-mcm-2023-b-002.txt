## Mathematical Specification

Let \(x_j\in\{0,1\}\) indicate adoption/intensity of policy \(j\), and \(s\) index scenarios. Let \(z_i\) denote zone-level allocations and \(y_{ik}\) network flows.

For scenario \(s\), maximize the vector

\[
\left[
B_s(x,z),\; W_s(x,z),\; R_s(x,z),\; -C_s(x,z),\; -F_s(x,z)
\right],
\]

where \(B\) is biodiversity/connectivity, \(W\) community welfare, \(R\) sustainable revenue, \(C\) implementation cost, and \(F\) conflict or enforcement risk.

Subject to

\[
\sum_j c_jx_j+\sum_i c_i z_i\leq \text{budget},
\]

\[
0\le z_i\le \text{capacity}_i,\qquad
0\le y_{ik}\le \text{edge capacity}_{ik},
\]

\[
\text{ecological thresholds}_s(x,z)\geq \tau_s,
\]

plus land-use, staffing, legal, and conservation constraints supplied by the problem. Generate the Pareto frontier, then apply a documented decision rule or stakeholder preference set.
