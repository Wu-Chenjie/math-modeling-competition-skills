2. Exposure and response variables are measured on compatible spatial units.
3. Potential confounders such as population density, urbanization, weather, season, socioeconomic status, and industrial activity can be observed or proxied.

## Candidate Models

**Spatial track**

- Baseline: descriptive maps, quantiles, exceedance fractions, and spatial autocorrelation.
- Candidate A: generalized additive model (GAM) with spatial smooth \(f(\text{longitude},\text{latitude})\) and temporal smooth \(g(t)\).
- Candidate B: Bayesian hierarchical spatial model with structured spatial effect and region-level random effects.

**Effect track**

- Baseline: adjusted linear/log-link regression with prespecified covariates.
- Candidate A: multilevel regression,
  \[
  h_{it}=\beta_0+\beta_1L_{it}+\boldsymbol{\gamma}^{T}\mathbf X_{it}+u_i+v_t+\epsilon_{it}.
  \]
- Candidate B: difference-in-differences or interrupted time-series model where credible intervention timing exists.

**Scenario track**

- Baseline: proportional reduction model \(L'=(1-r)L\).
- Candidate: constrained counterfactual simulation over intervention levers \(r\), with uncertainty propagated from the fitted exposure-response model.

## Math Specification

Let \(L(s,t)\) denote light-pollution intensity at location \(s\), time \(t\); \(Y(s,t)\) an environmental, health, or safety response; and \(\mathbf X(s,t)\) observed confounders.

Spatial model:
\[
\eta(s,t)=\alpha+\mathbf b^\top\mathbf X(s,t)+f_s(s)+f_t(t),\qquad
L(s,t)\sim p(\cdot\mid\eta(s,t)).
\]

Effect model:
\[
g\{E[Y(s,t)]\}=\theta_0+\theta_1L(s,t)+\boldsymbol{\theta}_X^\top\mathbf X(s,t)+u_{\text{region}}+v_t.
\]

Scenario \(k\) produces \(L_k(s,t)\). For a minimization objective,
\[
J_k=w_L\,\overline{L_k}+w_C\,C_k+w_R\,R_k,
\]
subject to budget, minimum service illumination, safety, and implementation constraints. Weights must be elicited or reported as ranges; arbitrary fixed weights are unacceptable.
