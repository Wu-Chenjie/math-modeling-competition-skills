## Boundary for reuse

Reusable: trigger/non-trigger contracts, focused responsibilities, human decision gates, source-linked artifacts, independent review axes, and progressive disclosure. Not reusable as math content: software domain-modeling vocabulary, coding-only TDD assumptions, or repository-specific tracker setup.
